# North Star

The durable product direction is to help clinicians understand a person's
measured health, see what is missing, and discuss meaningful change with
confidence in what the evidence supports.

Start with one musculoskeletal-focused measurement-review workflow. Success is
correct, independent review of measurements, missing inputs, FI and its
denominator, followed by a concise export. GOAL.md's first usability target is
four of five intended users completing the task within five minutes, with all
five recognizing the unvalidated age and clinical-advice boundary. These targets
have not been demonstrated.

Measurement provenance, honest comparisons, privacy, and independent evidence
take priority over adding scores. Numeric system ages require their own evidence
and qualified approval; a measurement-only product remains a valid outcome.

`NORTHSTAR.md` holds the durable direction. `GOAL.md` retains the acceptance
contract, `ROADMAP.md` the ordered IR0-IR7 plan, and `EVAL.md` the evidence ledger.
This documentation milestone closes no IR gate. E-005 remains blocked.
