# 016 - Trustworthy research showcase goal

Status: active goal shaped on 2026-09-10; implementation and acceptance open.

T1 turns the existing IR0-IR3 reviewer-package milestone into one execution
contract. It requires a clear first screen, personally inspected desktop/mobile
states, consistent traceable claims, measurement-first reports, ownership and
working evidence links, a verified published candidate and the existing human
workflow/accessibility review. Clinical E-005 remains blocked.

Direct inspection found older live HTML, numeric development age on the live
demo, missing live research report and mobile overflow. Local improvements exist,
but citation/metadata density and large withheld cards still dominate key views.
The full local verifier passed 20/20. This is software evidence, not visual,
clinical or final-release approval.

Official seca, InsideTracker and Dalhousie sources inform evidence navigation,
method attribution and intended-user clarity. Their validation and marketing
claims do not transfer to this software. No competitors' logged-in workflow
or clinical equivalence was assessed.
