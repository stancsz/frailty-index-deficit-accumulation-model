# Real public-data intake, 2026-09-11

were retrieved locally for review. Raw files remain outside this repository.

is [`REAL_DATA_INTAKE_RECEIPT_2026-09-11.json`](REAL_DATA_INTAKE_RECEIPT_2026-09-11.json).

[`CATEGORY_DATA_RECEIPT_2026-09-11.json`](CATEGORY_DATA_RECEIPT_2026-09-11.json)

The joint category now also has an explicit older direct-measurement source:
the official NHANES III `xr.dat` file contains 2,589 fixed-width participant
records with bilateral Kellgren-Lawrence, osteophyte, sclerosis,
chondrocalcinosis, and knee-replacement fields. The parser follows the
official [`xr.sas`](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/data/nhanes3/11a/xr.sas) layout
and records the source hash in the intake receipt. This is a separate
1991–1994, age-eligible radiograph sample, not a claim that the 2011–2012
questionnaire sample has a joint exam. Cycle harmonization, eligibility
handling, sentinel interpretation, and clinical mapping remain pending.

Joint coverage also now includes the official NHANES 2009–2010 `ARX_F.XPT`
arthritis body-measures file: 5,001 records with occiput-to-wall distance,
chest expansion, and lumbar-flexion measurements. CDC defines these as
clinical spinal-mobility tests for participants aged 20–69. This provides a
more contemporary direct mobility source, while remaining a one-cycle,
protocol-specific measurement set that still requires harmonization and review.

The same 2009–2010 cycle also provides `ARQ_F.XPT`, a 5,106-record trained
interviewer questionnaire covering chronic back or neck pain, inflammatory
back-pain indicators, heel or tendon symptoms, and related inflammatory
conditions. These symptom fields are retained as questionnaire context and
are not substituted for the direct mobility measurements.

Skin health now also has the official NHANES 2003–2004 `DEX_C.XPT`
dermatology examination file: 2,992 records with technician image-capture
status and consensus dermatologist readings for Fitzpatrick skin type, hand
dermatitis, psoriasis, and lesion locations. CDC documents the protocol as
four standardized digital images read by two dermatologists with consensus
review. This is direct examination evidence for adults aged 20–59 in that
cycle, not a validated skin-health score or a contemporary all-age cohort.

This is genuine public-use source data, not a synthetic fixture. It remains
only an intake receipt: no join, sentinel interpretation, quality filtering,
survey-variance calculation, reference profile, model fit, or clinical claim
has been approved or performed.

Body composition and muscle/function now have real source fields available for
reviewed mapping. The Physical Functioning file adds adult walking, standing,
work-limitation, and equipment variables for joint/function coverage. Additional
real component files now cover blood, blood
pressure, glucose/diabetes history, sleep, physical activity, smoking, alcohol,
depression-history, DXA bone-density/body-fat distribution, cognitive testing,
full dermatology questionnaire, spirometry, lipid laboratory fields, expanded CBC
differential/counts, kidney chemistries, liver enzymes, the full depression
screener, objective wrist-monitor movement/sleep summaries, and legacy BIA fields.
Every current category now has at least
one real public source field or real measured proxy in the intake inventory.
This does not mean every category has a validated construct: joint health now
has one direct radiographic source plus modern history/function proxies, but
cycle harmonization and clinical mapping are pending; skin health now has a
direct dermatology image-reading source plus modern questionnaire fields;
cardiorespiratory coverage now includes direct
spirometry but remains unvalidated. Joint coverage now includes direct
spinal-mobility and radiographic sources, plus arthritis/gout history and the
adult functional-difficulty item set; the cycles remain separate and the
construct is not yet validated.
Mental-health coverage now includes the full depression screener item set plus
current-health-status measures for recent poor mental-health days, activity
limitation, pain-related interference, and anxiety days. These are population
questionnaire measures, not a clinical diagnosis. Kidney, liver, blood, and immune categories
now use multiple analytes rather than a single laboratory proxy. Wrist-monitor day summaries contain repeated
participant rows, so participant-level aggregation and quality filtering remain
required. Immune/inflammatory coverage now also includes the official
2009–2010 C-reactive protein laboratory file (`CRP_F.XPT`, `LBXCRP`) alongside
the CBC differential. This is an inflammatory biomarker, not a disease
diagnosis, and requires cycle, assay, missingness, and survey-weight review.
Category-specific protocols, reference bands, and review remain
pending. Numeric ages remain withheld for every category.

The receipt also maps additional observed fields from the existing direct
components: weight, BMI, waist, arm circumference, and sagittal-abdominal
measurements for body composition; total and regional DXA bone mass, density,
fat, and spine fields; individual grip trials and status for muscle; and
additional cognitive status, recall, story, and animal-naming fields. These
expanded fields improve source completeness but do not authorize a composite
score or clinical interpretation.

Cardiovascular and blood coverage now also maps 2011–2012 apolipoprotein B
(`APOB_G.XPT`, `LBXAPB`, `LBDAPBSI`) alongside total cholesterol, triglyceride,
LDL, blood-pressure, and CBC fields. The apolipoprotein source is a laboratory
subsample and requires its subsample weight and survey-design handling.

Cardiorespiratory coverage also maps the 2011–2012 exhaled nitric oxide
examination (`ENX_G.XPT`), including exam status, attempt count, trial values,
and the mean of two reproducible FENO measures. CDC defines this as an airway
inflammation marker for the documented 6–79 sample. It remains a measurement
source, not an asthma or COPD diagnosis.

Kidney coverage now also maps the official 2011–2012 urine albumin/creatinine
component (`ALB_CR_G.XPT`): albumin and creatinine in both reported units plus
the albumin-to-creatinine ratio, alongside serum creatinine, BUN, and uric acid.
The urine file’s detection-limit and sample-design rules remain to be reviewed
before any clinical interpretation.

Liver coverage now also includes the official 2017–March 2020 pre-pandemic
transient-elastography file (`P_LUX.XPT`), with 10,409 records containing exam
status, retained-measure count, liver stiffness, stiffness quality, and
controlled attenuation parameter measures. This is a later-cycle direct
FibroScan source and is deliberately not merged into the 2011–2012 cohort.

Fluid and cellular coverage now maps the BIA examination status, fit and
quality fields, resistance/reactance and phase-related fields, total body
water, intracellular water, fat-free mass, fat mass, and percent fat. The BIA
source remains the 2003–2004 component and requires device-era transfer and
quality review before comparison with other components.

The numeric summary gives non-missing `n`, mean, median, minimum, and maximum
for observed numeric source fields. It is an unfiltered source distribution,
not a clinical reference interval, and must not be used to set cutoffs or
interpret an individual.

The [`CATEGORY_QUALITY_RECEIPT_2026-09-11.json`](CATEGORY_QUALITY_RECEIPT_2026-09-11.json)
adds a field-level missingness and candidate-special-code audit for all 17
primary categories. It counts values such as 7, 9, 77, 99, 777, 999, 9999,
and 99999 where they occur, but removes nothing and does not declare any code
invalid. Official codebook review, cycle-specific eligibility, survey weights,
and analytic cleaning remain required.
The representative [`NHANES_CODEBOOK_REVIEW_2026-09-11.json`](NHANES_CODEBOOK_REVIEW_2026-09-11.json)
records source-backed semantics for questionnaire, monitor, laboratory,
anthropometry, and elastography fields. It explicitly shows that a candidate
code is not automatically invalid: CDC documents `PAXVMD=999` within its valid
0–1440-minute range, while `DPQ010=7/9` are refused/don't-know codes.

The same non-destructive audit is also generated for the 2013–2014 receipt
and the 2017–2018 liver receipt as
`CATEGORY_QUALITY_RECEIPT_2013_2014_2026-09-11.json` and
`CATEGORY_QUALITY_RECEIPT_2017_2018_LIVER_2026-09-11.json`.

independently records 99 official CDC codebook/layout URLs across eight source
